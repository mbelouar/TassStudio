2. Information collected and its sources

We receive information you provide directly, information generated when you use our services, and information from platform and advertising providers involved in delivering those services. Advertising SDKs can collect information directly from your device, even where TASS STUDIO does not itself store a copy.

- Contact and support information: your name, email address, correspondence, and any information you choose to include in an enquiry, support request, or privacy request. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.
- Device and network identifiers: IP address, mobile advertising identifiers, where permitted; other app or device identifiers available to an enabled SDK; device model, operating system, language, time zone, app version, and connection information.
- App activity and advertising information: session and usage information, ad requests, impressions, clicks, video views or completions, and associated measurement events. Enabled advertising SDKs may process diagnostic and performance information to deliver and troubleshoot ads.
- General location: IP addresses can be used by advertising providers to estimate a country or general area. This is different from GPS location. Permission-based approximate or precise location is collected by an SDK only if the Application requests the relevant permission, you grant it, and that feature is enabled. Permission requests explain the purpose at the point of collection.
- Local game information: Gem Expedition stores game progress, balances, reward-pack progress and preferences on the device. Its current advertising integration does not send a developer-provided user identifier or purchase events. The game does not offer real-money purchases.
- Mahjong local game information: Mahjong Solitaire Match Puzzle stores player profile selections, game progress, scores, booster and ad-completion progress, and sound/music preferences on the device. Its current integration does not supply a developer-defined user ID or purchase events to Appodeal.
- Privacy preferences: consent choices, opt-out requests, permission status, and records needed to honour and demonstrate those choices.
- Website information: hosting and network providers may process IP addresses, requested URLs, browser information, and access or security logs when serving the site. Our website stores your selected colour theme locally under the key tass-theme. The contact form prepares an email in your email application; sending that email shares the form contents with us and the email providers involved.

An advertising identifier or IP address can be personal information even if it does not contain your name.

Super Fortune Garuda Slots

Super Fortune Garuda Slots is an Android game from TASS STUDIO. Spins and rewards use virtual coins. These coins have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money.

What the game saves. Your device stores your virtual coin balance, game progress, daily rewards, rewarded-ad progress, and sound and music settings. This lets you pick up where you left off. Clearing the app’s storage in Android settings removes the game’s local save, including the coin balance.

Advertising. The Google Play release uses Appodeal to manage ad requests and Google AdMob as an advertising partner. Development and QA versions use test ads. Ad placements include a bottom banner, occasional full-screen ads, and optional videos for in-game rewards. When a video finishes, the game uses the completion signal to update your reward progress.

Where a privacy form is required, the advertising SDK can ask for your choices. You can revisit those choices using Ad privacy choices in Settings when that option is available.

The active ad provider may receive your IP address, device and app information, advertising ID where available, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure their performance and troubleshoot problems. See the Appodeal Privacy Policy (https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy/) and Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details. For questions about your game data, contact tasstudio@proton.me.

- Contextual advertising, ad measurement, and delivery: the applicable lawful basis for the specific activity, including consent for device storage or access where required; legitimate interests only where permitted and after considering your rights.

Providing information in an enquiry is voluntary, but we may be unable to answer without contact details or resolve a problem without relevant information. We do not treat downloading an Application, visiting the website, or reading this policy as consent to personalised advertising.
